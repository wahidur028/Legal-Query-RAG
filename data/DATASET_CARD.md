## Required provenance fields for a research release

For each document, record a stable identifier, title, issuing authority, jurisdiction, publication date, retrieval URL, retrieval date, checksum, license or public-domain basis, preprocessing steps, and the split or experimental role.

## Privacy and legal use

Do not add privileged, confidential, copyrighted, or personally sensitive legal records unless you have authority to process and redistribute them. The application copies uploaded files into `.runtime/`, which is excluded from Git.
